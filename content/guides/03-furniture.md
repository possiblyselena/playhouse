## Quick Guide

Here's a quick run-through on how I made this oven with Onshape. It will teach you the basic tools to make basically whatever you want!

![animal crossing oven](/guide/assets/image-9.png)

1. Choose the top plane and create a sketch with the shape of the bottom. I knew that the round part of the oven on top needs to be smaller, so I didn't add it to my inital sketch.

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-6.png)

2. I extruded that and then added a sketch ontop for the round part. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-10.png)

3. I extruded that again and used the fillet tool to round out the top. Now it looks more like it!

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-8.png)

4. I made another sketch to make a shape to seperate the round part from the rest of the body.

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-11.png)

5. I used extrude remove to cut out the shape.

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-12.png)

6. Made another sketch on the round part to cut out and fillet the inner corners to curve with the outside. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-14.png)

I've added to our assembly to see what it looks like so far! What's neat is that I can keep working on my part and it automatically updates in my assembly. So I can keep making changes and it will stay in that position!

![3D assembly model](/Assembly.gltf)

It is really useful if you're switching between your assembly and furniture design.
