This might seem complicated, but once you're in the flow you'll make progress in no time!

Here's a quick run-through on how I made this oven with Onshape. It will teach you the basic tools to make basically whatever you want!

Animal crossing has a lot of good furniture to reference !
![alt text](/guide/assets/image-9.png)

1. Choose the top plane and create a sketch with the shape of the bottom. I knew that the round part of the oven on top needs to be smaller, so I didn't add it to my inital sketch.

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-6.png)

2. I extruded that and then added a sketch ontop for the round part. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-7.png)

3. I extruded that again and used the fillet tool to round out the top. Now it looks more like it!

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-8.png)
