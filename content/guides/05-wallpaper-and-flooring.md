
# Designing Wallpaper and Flooring!

## This is a Canva link for the template! 

https://canva.link/dlcbb9debe3bzqv

With this document you can easily drag and drop your pictures/designs and adjust them to fit the canvas. Once you're finished you can print, cut, and glue on your walls.

## Make sure you do it BEFORE you attach your walls together to avoid the headache.

If you're planning to draw your own, make sure you use [Lapse](https://lapse.hackclub.com/) to track your art hours. Keep in mind that art hours cannot exceed 25% of your total Hackatime hours.
