## Assembly

Once you've created enough parts to make a box, it's time to assemble!

1. Head to insert, you'll see all your parts you made in this document. For now, we're going to start with our box.

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-16.png)

2. Select all your parts and press the green check mark to add them to your assembly. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-17.png)

How mating works in Onshape is that you have mate connectors, and you can think of them as pinpoints of where things are stuck together. With those mate connectors, you can set a mate to join them together. Mate connectors have a XYZ axis associated with them and you make sure that the mate connectors between two objects are oriented properly.

3. Select the mate connector and I'm going to put one in the very corner of the bottom plate and the corner of the side plate. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-19.png)

You might need to play around wuth your cursor to get the axis to align. But you can also flip the primary axis (blue) and reorient secondary axis (red and yellow) to help. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-20.png)

My parts were laying flat and I rotated my side plate to get a better look on where it's going. 

4. Select the fasten mate and we're going to select both the mate connecters we just put. 

![alt text](/guide/assets/image-21.png)

## Then repeat the process for the rest of the box! 

Once you have your box, it's pretty much the same process for furniture.

If you want to place a mate connector in a specific area, you can do it manually! I placed this mate connector on the floor, and select move. You can set the distance on each axis.
![alt text](/guide/assets/image-22.png)

I'm going to move my mate connector forward and to the left, then add my counter top!
